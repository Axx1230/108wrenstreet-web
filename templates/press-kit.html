CASE FILE #143
PRODUCTION DOSSIER

FULL SYNOPSIS

At 108 Wren Street, memories can be removed. A client arrives at the Memory Services clinic ready to surrender the moments that keep pulling him back. But the fragments are bound to more than pain: a shared joke, a small confession, a promise made beneath an oak tree. As the extraction moves between the controlled present and the warmth of recollection, the procedure asks whether a life can be edited without changing the person who lived it.

“If you could edit the pain out of your past, would you still recognise the person left behind?”JOSH OREMULE / DIRECTOR’S STATEMENT

PRODUCTION NOTES

01 CAMERA ARRI ALEXA MINI / VINTAGE ANAMORPHIC+

Intimate, tactile images shaped by available light and vintage anamorphic glass. The camera stays close to the details memory leaves behind.

02 SOUND DESIGN AMBISONICS / SUBJECTIVE SPACE+

Spatial sound places room tone, breath and recurring details around the listener, shifting between clinical precision and the distortion of recollection.

03 EDITING NOAH LEWIS / MEMORY RHYTHM+

Fragments overlap and return with changed meaning, letting the emotional edit follow the logic of remembering.

04 COLOUR AMBER / CLINICAL CYAN+

Warm amber tones anchor the remembered world; clinical cyan frames the precise, cool present of the memory service.
